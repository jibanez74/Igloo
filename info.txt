I fixed the rename-reconciliation issue in the movie scanner.

The scanner now keeps track of files it actually processed during the scan, and `reconcileMissingMovies()` uses that to try a rename match before deleting anything. When it finds a strong match, it preserves the original movie row and ID, reassigns the new path onto that row, deletes the temporary duplicate row, and re-runs scanner processing on the preserved record so streams/chapters and TMDB-derived data stay in sync.
